# InkVox

InkVox is a lightweight, voice-first input method created for Android e-ink devices. It helps readers effortlessly capture quick annotations, thoughts, and reading notes through speech rather than typing on a sluggish virtual keyboard.

## Why InkVox

Typing on e-ink devices can be cumbersome. Full-sized on-screen keyboards take up valuable page space, trigger heavy screen redraws, and can cause low-power e-readers to lag and warm up.

InkVox offers a calmer, more natural alternative: speak your thought, and have it appear directly in your reading or note-taking app.

## Highlights

- **One-Tap Dictation**: Tap to start speaking, tap again to finish. Your transcribed words are inserted right where your cursor is.
- **E-Ink Friendly Layout**: High-contrast, black-and-white interface designed specifically for e-paper screens, with zero distracting animations or rapid screen flickering.
- **Quiet and Lightweight**: Runs quietly in the background without draining battery or warming up your device.
- **Privacy by Default**: Audio recordings and recognized text are never stored locally or collected for diagnostics.
- **Just What You Need**: Keeps the interface simple with only essential controls—record, cancel, backspace, and a quick toggle to switch back to your standard keyboard when fine editing is needed.

## Getting Started

### Initial Setup

1. Open the InkVox app on your device.
2. Enter your speech service credentials and save your configuration.
3. Tap **Enable InkVox** to toggle the input method on in Android system settings.
4. Tap **Select InkVox** to choose InkVox as your active keyboard.

### Daily Use

1. Tap on any text box in your reading or note app.
2. When the InkVox panel appears, tap **Start Recording** (grant microphone permission on first use).
3. Speak your note, then tap **Stop Recording** when you are done.
4. The transcribed text will appear directly at your cursor position.
